# CC Major-Planning Advisor Agent (`CCAdvisorAgent`)

Repo: [github.com/eosterloh/CCAdvisorAgent](https://github.com/eosterloh/CCAdvisorAgent) · March–May 2026 · C++.

Agentic context-managed web scraper and academic advisor built in C++ for Colorado College students. Scrapes department catalog pages on demand, uses tool-calling to pull prerequisite and major-requirement pages into managed context, plans multi-block course paths, and escalates ambiguous cases to human advisors.
